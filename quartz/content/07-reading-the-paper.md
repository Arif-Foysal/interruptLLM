# Module 07 — Reading the Paper

> This module walks you through the InterruptLLM paper section by section, with explanations, visualizations, and critical questions.

## Why this module matters

This is the culmination of the curriculum. You apply everything from Modules 1–6 to read the actual paper deeply.

## How to use this module

1. Read the corresponding section of the paper (`paper/main.tex` or `paper/main.pdf`).
2. Read the matching note here.
3. Answer the check-your-understanding questions.
4. Do the exercises.
5. After finishing all sections, write your own critique.

## Key takeaways

- The paper makes a clear problem statement and solution.
- The design is built on PagedAttention's block tables.
- The evaluation is simulation-based with real GPU calibration.
- The main result is strong, but limitations are acknowledged.
